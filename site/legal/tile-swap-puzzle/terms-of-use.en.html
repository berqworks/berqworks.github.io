The App can be downloaded for free. Some puzzle categories are free (currently Animals, Sea Life, Vehicles and Toys); the other categories are paid.

The Puzzle of the Week lets you play one puzzle from a locked paid category for free each week. It changes every Monday, by the date on your device, and can be played until Sunday. It does not unlock the rest of that category.

We may change which content is free in future versions. A change never takes away content that you have already bought.

5. In-app purchases

All purchases are optional, one-time and non-consumable. The App has no subscriptions, no virtual currency and no random-reward items (loot boxes).

  • Unlock All: permanently unlocks all categories, including paid categories that we add to the App later.

Seasonal categories (for example Christmas or Halloween) are highlighted during their season, but they can be bought at any time of the year and stay unlocked permanently.
