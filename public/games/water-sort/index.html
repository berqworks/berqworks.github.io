Water Sort: Color Atlas

Pour each color into its own vessel and bring faded scenes back to color.

Coming soon to Google Play

How to play

Water Sort: Color Atlas is a calm sorting puzzle. Pour colored liquids between tubes until every color is in a vessel of its own. Each level you finish brings color back to one part of a scene in the Atlas, and every 20 levels a whole scene is complete.

Highlights

  • The Color Atlas

    50 scenes, 1,000 levels. Each level colors one of a scene's 20 areas; every 20 levels a scene is complete.

  • A new center vessel every level

    The large vessel in the middle holds 8 units and takes one of 30 shapes that hint at what you are coloring: lighthouse, whale, bell, owl…

  • Every level can be solved

    Levels are checked by a solver before release. Hints follow a real solution, and the game tells you plainly when there is no way out from where you are.

  • Color aid

    Each of the 12 colors has its own symbol, with an optional pattern. Offered from level 6, and always in Settings.

  • Daily puzzle

    A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Keep a streak for extra Pigment on days 3 and 7.

  • No timer, no lives

    Play at your own pace. There is no countdown and no energy system.
