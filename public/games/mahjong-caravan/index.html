Mahjong Caravan

A calm mahjong solitaire with no time limit and no penalties, where every board you're dealt can be solved.

Coming soon to Google Play

How to play

Match pairs of free tiles until the board is empty. Undo as often as you like, and if you get stuck, shuffle what's left; the board is rebuilt so it can still be solved. Play a 400-level journey or pick any of 46 layouts.

Highlights

  • Every board can be solved

    Boards are built by playing a solution in reverse, so there is always a way through. A shuffle uses the same method on the tiles that are left.

  • No time pressure, no penalties

    The clock only decides how many stars you get. No board ends because time ran out, and a wrong pair costs nothing.

  • Unlimited undo

    Take back as many moves as you want, shuffles included.

  • 400 levels, 46 layouts

    A journey that grows from 30-tile shapes to full 144-tile boards, split into eight chapters of 50 levels. In free play, pick any of the 46 layouts.

  • Easy on the eyes

    Large tiles and text, a high-contrast theme, corner labels on tiles, free-tile highlighting, left-handed mode and reduced motion.
