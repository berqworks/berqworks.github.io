This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform. It tells users of the Android game Mahjong Caravan: Tile Journey ("the Game") how their personal data is processed. The full details are in the Privacy Policy. If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

In short

  • The Game has no accounts; we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The Game contains no analytics or usage-statistics SDK, and it does not report to us how you play.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob and the purchase by Google Play. These providers' servers are located outside Türkiye.
  • To use your rights under Article 11 of KVKK, write to berqworks@gmail.com.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Showing and measuring ads and preventing ad fraud; personalizing ads where this is allowed.
  • Carrying out and recognising the "Remove ads" purchase and handling refunds.
  • Ensuring information security.
  • Answering support requests and fixing reported bugs.
  • Meeting accounting, tax and other legal obligations, and protecting our rights in legal disputes.

4. Method of collection and legal grounds

Your personal data is collected electronically: automatically through the Google libraries used in the Game, from Google Play's developer reports, and from emails you send us. The legal grounds are:

  • Advertising data: our legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads, measuring those ads and preventing fraud (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Purchase and order records: the formation and performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)) and our legal obligations (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Support correspondence and bug reports: our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)); where the request concerns a purchase, the performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)); and the establishment, exercise or protection of a right (KVKK Art. 5(2)(e)).

5. Transfer of personal data

Your personal data is transferred for the purposes above, and only as far as necessary, to these recipients:

  • Google LLC, Google Ireland Limited and their affiliates: Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchase and order records), email hosting (Gmail).
  • GitHub, Inc. and Netlify, Inc.: hosting our website.
  • Authorised public institutions and courts: only where a legal obligation or the protection of a right requires it.

Transfers abroad. Because these providers' servers are located abroad, mainly in the United States and the European Union, your personal data is transferred abroad. Transfers take place in line with the conditions set out in Article 9 of KVKK.

6. Retention periods

  • Advertising and purchase data is kept by Google for the periods set out in Google's own policies.
  • Order records kept for accounting and tax purposes are kept for as long as the applicable law requires.
  • Support correspondence and bug reports are deleted six months after the request has been closed, unless a legal obligation or a legal claim requires us to keep them longer.
  • Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Data whose retention period has ended is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to apply to the data controller to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information about the processing,
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred,
  • ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected,
  • ask for the data to be erased or destroyed under the conditions of Article 7 of KVKK,
  • ask for corrections, erasures and destructions to be notified to the third parties who received the data,
  • object to a result to your detriment that arises from analysis of the data solely by automated systems,
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can limit the use of the advertising ID by resetting or deleting it in your device's settings. You can switch off full-screen ads with the "Remove ads" purchase; rewarded ads are shown only if you choose to watch them.

8. How to apply

To use your rights, send your request in writing, in Turkish and in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Applications to the Data Controller, to the address in section 1, or email it to berqworks@gmail.com from an email address you have previously given us and that is recorded in our system. Include your name and surname, your signature (for a written application), your Turkish identity number if you are a citizen of Türkiye (or, if not, your nationality and passport or identity number), your address or email address for notifications, and the subject of your request.

Because the Game has no accounts, game data is not linked to your name; telling us which data your request concerns (for example an email you sent or an order number) speeds things up. We will conclude your request free of charge as soon as possible and within thirty days at the latest; if the action requires an additional cost, the fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged.

If your application is rejected, if you find our answer insufficient or if we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within thirty days of learning our answer and in any case within sixty days of your application.
