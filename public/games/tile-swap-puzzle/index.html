Tile Swap

A picture puzzle for young children with no timer: swap the tiles until the picture is whole again.

Coming soon to Google Play

How to play

Tile Swap is a picture puzzle for young children. A picture is cut into squares and shuffled. Your child taps or drags two pieces to swap them, and each piece clicks into place when it lands where it belongs. There are 180 pictures in 18 categories, with no timer and no ads.

Highlights

  • Swap, drag, done

    Tap two pieces or drag one onto another. Pieces that land in the right spot lock in with a tick.

  • No timer, no ads

    There is no clock on any board, and the game shows no ads at all.

  • 18 categories, 180 pictures

    10 pictures in each category. Animals, Sea Life, Vehicles and Toys are free.

  • A guiding hand

    Until the first puzzle is solved, a hand shows each next move. No reading needed.

  • Hear the picture's name

    The finished picture's name (lion, rocket…) is shown and read aloud in the game's language.

  • Parental gate

    Purchases, the Parents area and outside links open only after an adult taps a spelled-out number sequence. Kids see "Ask a grown-up", never a price.
