This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in the Android game Tile Swap: Kids Puzzle Games (shown inside the game as "Tile Swap Puzzle"; Google Play package com.berqworks.tileswappuzzle), called "the App" below. The App is a picture puzzle game for young children and their families. It is published on Google Play by BerqWorks ("we", "us"). This policy is written for parents and guardians.

We built the App to work with as little personal information as possible.

In short

  • The App has no accounts. It never asks for a name, an email address, an age or any other personal information.
  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We run no servers, and the App does not send us anything automatically.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. It does not use the advertising ID.
  • Purchases are optional and are handled by Google Play. They can be started only after a parent check, and we never see payment card details.
  • Reminders are optional and are created on the device itself. They are not push notifications.
  • We receive personal information only if you contact us or leave a public review, and, if you buy something, as limited order records from Google Play that contain no name, email address or card details.

3. Information that stays on your device

Game data

The App keeps one small save file in its private storage on your device. It contains:

  • Progress: completed puzzles, the best star rating for each puzzle, expert-level badges, and the category, picture and difficulty you played last.
  • Settings: sound-effect and music volume, language, whether expert levels are switched on, and your choice about reminders.
  • Tutorial: whether the guided first puzzle has been finished.
  • Purchases: which categories are unlocked and whether "Unlock All" is owned. Google Play remains the source of truth; the App checks it again every time it starts.
  • Rating: whether a parent has already opened the App's rating page, so that the App stops asking.
  • Puzzle of the Week: the current week and the picture chosen for it.

The save file contains no name, email address, age, location or device identifier. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Technical logs

The App writes technical log files (five at most) to its private storage. They record things such as the game engine version, the graphics driver and graphics chip, whether the store could be loaded, reminder scheduling, warnings and errors. They never contain a complete purchase token, a name, an email address or an account identifier. Logs are not sent anywhere automatically. A short extract leaves your device only if you decide to email it to us (section 4).

Information the App reads from the device

The App reads the following information. It does not store it and does not send it to us:

  • the system language, to choose the game's language the first time it starts;
  • the local date and time, for the Puzzle of the Week, seasonal categories and reminders;
  • the device model, Android version and screen resolution, which are shown only in a hidden diagnostics panel and are added to a support email if a parent chooses to send one;
  • the status of the network connection, which the purchase library checks.

4. Information you choose to send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name shown by your email account, and whatever you write or attach. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report.

The App's "For Parents" area contains a hidden diagnostics panel for support requests. It can be opened only after the parent check, by tapping the version number seven times. Its "Send by Email" button opens a pre-filled message to our support address in your own email app. The message contains the App and game engine versions, the operating system, the device model, the screen resolution, the App and system language, the status of the store connection, and the most recent lines of the App's technical log from the current and the previous session. A "Copy Log to Clipboard" button copies the same information so that you can paste it yourself.

Nothing is sent unless you press "send" in your email app, and you can read, change or delete any part of the message before sending it. The App itself never sends an email.

Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). We delete support emails six months after the request has been closed (section 13). Please do not send us information about your child that we do not need to help you.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers optional one-time purchases: unlocking puzzle categories and "Unlock All". They are sold through Google Play Billing, and the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see, collect or store payment card details. Google processes payment information under its own Privacy Policy.

To deliver what was bought, the App receives the product identifiers, the purchase status and a purchase token with its signature from Google Play, on your device. The App checks the signature on the device itself. We have no verification server, so none of this is sent to us, and the App does not send an account or user identifier to Google Play. The device stores only which products are owned. Purchases are synchronized with Google Play each time the App starts or returns to the foreground.

As the publisher, we can see order records in Google Play's developer reports: order number, date, product, price and tax, device model, and the buyer's country, city or region and postal code. These records contain no name, email address or card details. We use them for accounting and tax purposes and to handle support and refund requests.

6. No advertising

The App does not show ads. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK, and no other tracking technology, and it does not use the Android advertising ID. The permissions an app needs to read the advertising ID, and the permissions for the Android Privacy Sandbox advertising features (topics and attribution), are removed from the App.

7. Reminders

If you allow it, the App shows occasional reminders, for example when a new Puzzle of the Week is available, at most one per day. They are scheduled and shown by your device itself. They are not push notifications: no notification service, device token or server is involved, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Reminders contain no ads and no purchase offers, and they stop by themselves if the App has not been opened for about four weeks.

The App asks once whether you would like reminders. On Android 13 and later, the system then asks for the notification permission. You can switch reminders off at any time in the App's Settings or in your device's settings.

8. Reading aloud

When a puzzle is finished and in the sticker album, the App can read short texts aloud: a word of praise, the name of the picture and the name of a badge. To do this, it hands the text to the text-to-speech engine selected on your device. The App does not record sound, does not use the microphone and does not send the text anywhere itself. Some text-to-speech engines generate speech over the network; that depends on the engine and is subject to the engine's own terms.

9. Permissions

The App uses only the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access and network status (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used by the purchase library to communicate with Google Play. The App has no server of its own to contact.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): in-app purchases.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): the optional reminders. On Android 13 and later it is requested only if you answer "Yes" to the App's question.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): schedules the reminders again after the device restarts.
  • Background tasks (WAKE_LOCK): short background tasks of the reminder library.
  • An internal permission that Android libraries add automatically (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): it protects the App's own components and gives no access to user data.

The App never asks for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, media or files, phone, accounts, calendar or Bluetooth.

10. Children's privacy

The App is made for young children and takes part in Google Play's Families program. Because the App is directed to children, we treat every user as a child.

What is collected from children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App gives a child no way to hand any over: there are no accounts, no chat, no sharing features and no user-generated content, and the App never asks for a name, an age or contact details. Playing never depends on a child providing personal information.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party SDK that collects information from a child's device, and the App itself collects no persistent identifier. Purchases are made by a parent, after the parent check, through Google Play (section 5). For these reasons the App does not ask for verifiable parental consent before a child plays.

The parent check

Before anything that is meant for adults, the App shows a parent check: four digits are written out as words in the game's language and must be tapped in the right order on the App's own keypad. The check protects every purchase, the "For Parents" area (privacy policy, terms, rating, restoring purchases, resetting progress, the image credit link and the diagnostics panel) and the rating question that can appear after a puzzle. Every link out of the App can be opened only after this check.

Parents' choices and rights

As a parent or guardian, you can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about your child, ask us to delete it, and refuse its further collection or use. In practice we hold no information about children: the App sends us nothing, and we cannot identify the people who play it. If your child has emailed us, tell us and we will delete the message.

The puzzles work without an internet connection. Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored on the device.

Operators

The operator responsible for the App is BerqWorks (Seher Hatipoğlu; contact details in sections 1 and 18). No third-party operator collects information from children through the App. You can send us any question about the privacy practices of the App.

12. Legal bases and international transfers

Where the General Data Protection Regulation of the EU or the United Kingdom (GDPR) or the Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering requests and fixing problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). Where your request concerns a purchase, the performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Order records: the performance of the purchase contract and our legal obligations, such as accounting and tax rules (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).
  • Legal claims: establishing, exercising or defending legal claims (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(e)).

Personal information reaches us by electronic means only: through the emails you send and through Google Play's developer console. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, and we do not profile users.

International transfers. We are based in Türkiye, so information you send us is received and handled outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Google and GitHub process information on servers in other countries, including the United States. Google describes the safeguards it uses for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions, in its data transfer frameworks. Where Turkish law applies, transfers abroad take place within the framework of Article 9 of KVKK.

13. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it. "Reset Progress" in the "For Parents" area erases completed puzzles, stars, expert badges and the tutorial status; purchases and settings remain. Clearing the App's data in Android's settings or uninstalling the App erases everything the App stored, including its log files.
  • No cloud backup. Android's backup is switched off for the App, so its data is not copied to Google Drive. For the same reason, progress cannot be recovered after the App is uninstalled or on a new device. Purchases are restored from your Google account.
  • Support emails are deleted six months after the request has been closed, unless we must keep a message longer to meet a legal obligation or to deal with a legal claim.
  • Order records are kept by Google in Google Play's developer console. Where we keep copies for accounting and tax purposes, we keep them for as long as the applicable law requires.

To ask us to delete information that we hold, which in practice means an email you sent us, write to us using the details in section 18. We answer within 30 days.

14. Security

  • Game data and logs are kept in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read on a standard Android device.
  • We do not operate servers or databases that hold information about users.
  • The connections made by the purchase library are encrypted in transit.
  • Purchases are verified on the device with a cryptographic signature.
  • Support emails are kept in a mailbox to which only we have access.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keeping the amount of information to a minimum is our main safeguard.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over personal information about you and, as a parent or guardian, about your child.

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: you can ask for access to your personal data, for its correction or erasure, for the restriction of its processing and for data portability, and you can object to processing that is based on legitimate interests. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office).
  • Türkiye: under Article 11 of KVKK you can learn whether your personal data is processed; request information about the processing; learn its purpose and whether the data is used for that purpose; learn the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected; ask for data to be erased or destroyed; ask for these corrections and deletions to be notified to the third parties who received the data; object to a result to your detriment that arises from analysis solely by automated systems; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. If we reject your request, if you find our answer insufficient or if we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within thirty days of learning our answer and in any case within sixty days of your request.
  • United States: the rights of parents under COPPA are described in section 10.
  • Other countries: we honour the rights that your local law gives you.

How to use your rights. Email or write to us using the details in section 18. We answer free of charge within 30 days. Because the App has no accounts and sends us nothing, we usually cannot link a person to any data. In practice we can act on emails you have sent us and, if you give us the order number, on order records. We may ask for the information we need to confirm that a request really comes from you, for example a reply from the email address that the original message came from. For information processed by Google, you can also contact Google as described in its Privacy Policy.
