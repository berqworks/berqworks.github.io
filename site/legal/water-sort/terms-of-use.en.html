All purchases are optional. The Game has no subscriptions.

7. Internet connection and the daily puzzle

The Game needs an internet connection to play. Without a connection, a new level cannot be started; if the connection drops in the middle of a level, you can finish that level. Owners of the No Ads Pack can also play levels offline; purchases, rewarded ads and the daily puzzle need a connection for everyone.

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone each day. The day is determined by network time, independently of the device clock, in your local time zone. Keeping your streak earns extra Pigment; missing a day resets the streak. A daily puzzle that has been started can be finished even if the connection drops. We may limit attempts to abuse rewards by changing the device clock or time zone.

8. Game progress

They are lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its data or change your device, and we cannot restore them. One-time packs can be restored with the same Google account (section 5.4).
