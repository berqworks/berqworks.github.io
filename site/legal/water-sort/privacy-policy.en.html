This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in the Android game Water Sort: Color Atlas (Turkish title "Water Sort: Renk Atlası"; Google Play package com.berqworks.watersort), called "the Game" below. The Game is a puzzle game in which you pour colored liquids between vessels to sort them. It is meant for players aged 13 and over and is published on Google Play by BerqWorks ("we", "us").

We built the Game to work with as little personal information as possible.

In short

  • The Game has no accounts. It never asks for a name, an email address, a phone number or an age.
  • Your progress, Pigment balance and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted file. We run no servers.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played. Analytics stays off until you tap "Accept and continue" on the consent screen at first launch, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Usage statistics. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve them, Google processes device information, including the advertising ID. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown first.
  • Purchases are optional and are handled by Google Play. We never see payment card details.
  • Reminders are optional and are created on the device itself. They are not push notifications.

3. Information that stays on your device

Game data

The Game keeps an encrypted save file, and a backup copy of it, in its private storage on your device. It contains:

  • Progress: the level you have reached, the stars for each level, the state of an unfinished level (moves and remaining uses), and which tutorial steps you have seen.
  • Economy: your Pigment balance, boosters (Undo, Empty Tube, Hint) and how many rewarded ads were watched that day.
  • Cosmetics: the bottles, backgrounds and liquid effects you own and use.
  • Daily puzzle: the last completed day and your streak.
  • Purchases: whether you own the No Ads Pack and the Starter Pack. Google Play remains the source of truth; the Game checks it again every time it starts or returns to the foreground.
  • Consent: which policy version you accepted and when, and your answer to the reminder question.
  • Settings: sound-effect and music volume, vibration, language, color aid, reduced motion, reminders, usage statistics, and the hour at which you usually play, used to time reminders (the hour only, for example "19").
  • Ad and connection counters: how many levels have been finished since the last interstitial ad and when it was shown, and how many levels were played offline. They are used only to apply the ad frequency and connection rules.

The save file contains no name, email address, age, location or device identifier. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Information the Game reads from the device

The Game reads the following information. It does not send it to us:

  • the system language, to choose the Game's language the first time it starts;
  • the local time and time zone, to work out the day of the daily puzzle and the time of reminders;
  • the device model and Android version, which are added only to the email that Settings → Support opens (section 10);
  • the status of the network connection, which the Game and the ad and purchase libraries check.

4. Privacy consent

When the Game starts for the first time, it shows a consent screen with links to this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use and the KVKK Notice. The screen also says that the Game shows ads and collects usage statistics. Until you tap "Accept and continue", the Game sends no analytics events, does not start the ad SDK and makes no ad requests. If you tap "Decline", the Game cannot be played and can be closed.

Your consent is stored on your device together with the policy version number. If we make an important change to this policy that raises the version number, the Game asks you to accept the new version when it starts.

5. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To improve the Game, for example to see which levels are too hard, whether the economy is balanced or where an error occurs, we use Google's Firebase Analytics service. Analytics is off by default and is switched on only after you tap "Accept and continue" on the consent screen. You can switch it off at any time in Settings → Usage statistics; while it is off, no events are sent. Switching it off does not limit any feature of the Game.

Events that are sent

  • Levels: start, completion, restart, quitting and getting stuck; level number, number of moves, target number of moves, stars, duration, and the undos, hints and extra tubes used; the result of a hint.
  • Progress: completed pages, starting and finishing the daily puzzle, tutorial steps.
  • Economy and shop: Pigment earned and spent (amount, source, balance), boosters used, the shop being opened, cosmetics bought and equipped.
  • Purchases: a product being viewed, a purchase (product and price), a failed attempt and its reason, and an entitlement removed after a refund.
  • Ads: an ad being shown (format and placement), a rewarded ad being completed, an ad failing to load.
  • Other: app start, a level that could not start because there was no connection, the result of the notification permission request, the result of the privacy consent, settings changes (the setting's name and new value), and an error record when the picture on screen and the game logic disagree (level and vessel number).

Information Firebase adds automatically

Firebase also adds some information automatically: a random identifier specific to the app (the app instance ID), first-open and session information, engagement time, device model, Android version, app version, language, where the Game was installed from (the Google Play install referrer) and an approximate location derived from the IP address (country, region or city level).

Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising ID; this is switched off in the Game's configuration. The Game sets no user ID. Analytics data contains no name or contact details and is not used to identify you or to show you ads.

Google processes this data on our behalf and keeps it for the retention period set in our Firebase project (14 months at most). Details: Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads are shown

The Game is free and shows three kinds of ads from Google AdMob:

  • Banner: only at the bottom of the Atlas screen.
  • Interstitial (full-screen): after level 10, every few levels, when you finish a level and return to the Atlas. There are at least two minutes between two interstitials, and their number per session is limited. No interstitial is shown while a level is being played, after the daily puzzle or in purchase windows.
  • Rewarded: opens only if you tap a button (free Pigment in the shop, and doubling the reward at the end of a level), and gives only Pigment in return. Boosters are never given for watching ads.

If you own the No Ads Pack, banner and interstitial ads are neither shown nor requested; rewarded ads open only if you choose to watch one.

How ad requests are set up

  • The maximum ad content rating is "T" (Teen), and sensitive ad categories such as gambling are blocked in our AdMob account.
  • The Game is meant for players aged 13 and over; ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.
  • No other ad network and no ad mediation are used.

What Google may process

To deliver, measure and, where allowed, personalize ads, to prevent fraud and to keep its service secure, Google may process the following information from the device:

  • the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and Game version;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data, including crash logs.

Where your device supports them, Google's ad SDK may also use Android's advertising features (Privacy Sandbox: topics and attribution). Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information; in AdMob we see only aggregated reports that do not identify anyone.

Your choices

  • Consent form: in regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before loading ads. If you refuse personalized ads, non-personalized or limited ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (this row appears only where your region requires it).
  • Device settings: you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device's settings (depending on the device, Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No Ads Pack: turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Tapping an ad

Tapping an ad can open a web page or a Google Play page outside the Game. The sites and apps reached in this way have their own privacy practices.

7. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: Pigment packs, the Starter Pack and the No Ads Pack. They are sold through Google Play Billing, and the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see, collect or store payment card details. Google processes payment information under its own Privacy Policy.

To deliver what was bought, the Game receives the product identifiers, the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, on your device; it acknowledges or consumes the product and stores on the device which one-time products you own. We have no verification server, so none of this is sent to us, and the Game does not send an account or user identifier to Google Play. Purchases are synchronized with Google Play each time the Game starts or returns to the foreground; a pending purchase, or one completed in the background, is delivered at that point.

As the publisher, we can see order records in Google Play's developer reports: order number, date, product, price and tax, device model, and the buyer's country, city or region and postal code. These records contain no name, email address or card details. We use them for accounting and tax purposes and to handle support and refund requests. If usage statistics are on, purchase events (product and price) are also sent to Firebase (section 5).

8. Internet connection and network time

The Game needs an internet connection to play; owners of the No Ads Pack can also play offline. The daily puzzle needs a connection for everyone. To check the connection, the Game sends a short request to a Google server (www.google.com/generate_204). To work out the day of the daily puzzle independently of the device clock, it reads only the current time from the same server or, if that cannot be reached, from a Cloudflare server (www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace).

So that settings such as ad frequency, rewards and in-game prices can change without an update, and so that very old versions can be directed to update, the Game downloads a small settings file from our website (berqworks.github.io) when it starts. The site is hosted by GitHub.

These requests contain no information about the Game or about you. As with every internet request, these services see your IP address and the technical details of the request and handle them under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Cloudflare Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Reminders

At first launch, the Game asks whether you would like daily puzzle and streak reminders. If you agree, on Android 13 and later the system then asks for the notification permission. Reminders are scheduled on your device itself, with Android's scheduler: at most one per day, and none between 21:30 and 09:00. They arrive, for example, when a new daily puzzle is ready or when your streak is about to break. If the Game has not been opened for a week, reminders stop by themselves.

They are not push notifications: no notification service, device token or server is involved, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can switch reminders off at any time in Settings → Notifications or in your device's settings.

10. Information you choose to send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name shown by your email account, and whatever you write or attach. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report.

Settings → Support opens a pre-filled message to our support address in your own email app. The subject contains the Game version, and the body contains the device model and Android version, which help us find the problem faster. Nothing is sent unless you press "send" in your email app, and you can change or delete any part of the message before sending it. The Game itself never sends an email.

Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). We delete support emails six months after the request has been closed (section 15).

11. Permissions

The Game and the libraries it uses use these Android permissions:

  • Internet access and network status (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): ads, purchases, usage statistics, connection and time checks, and downloading the settings file.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): the optional reminders. On Android 13 and later it is requested only if you agree to reminders.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): schedules the reminders again after the device restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): haptic feedback; it can be switched off in Settings.
  • Google Play billing and licensing (com.android.vending.BILLING, CHECK_LICENSE): in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID and Android advertising features (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION): for Google AdMob (section 6). Firebase Analytics does not use the advertising ID.
  • Install referrer (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): lets Firebase learn where the Game was installed from.
  • Background tasks (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): short background tasks of the ad, analytics and reminder libraries. The Game's own code does not start a foreground service.
  • Permissions that libraries add automatically (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE, DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): added by Google's libraries; they give no access to your phone number, calls or personal data.

The Game never asks for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, media or files, accounts, calendar or Bluetooth. The exact-alarm and battery-optimization exemption permissions have been removed from the Game.

14. Legal bases and international transfers

Where the GDPR or the Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Usage statistics; withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.
  • Personalized ads and the advertising ID: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)). In regions where consent is required, it is collected through Google's consent form and can be changed in Settings → Privacy options; everywhere, it can be withdrawn in your device's ad settings.
  • Serving and measuring non-personalized ads and preventing fraud: our legitimate interest in funding a free game with advertising (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Purchases and order records: the performance of the purchase contract and our legal obligations, such as accounting and tax rules (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).
  • Connection and network time checks, settings file: providing the Game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of daily rewards (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering requests and fixing problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)); where your request concerns a purchase, the performance of a contract.
  • Legal claims: establishing, exercising or defending legal claims (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(e)).

Personal information is collected by electronic means: automatically through the Game, and through the emails you send us. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

International transfers. We are based in Türkiye, so information you send us is received and handled outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Google, Cloudflare, GitHub and Netlify process information on servers in other countries, including the United States. Google describes the safeguards it uses for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions, in its data transfer frameworks. Where Turkish law applies, transfers abroad take place within the framework of Article 9 of KVKK.

15. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Clearing the Game's data in Android's settings or uninstalling the Game erases everything the Game stored.
  • No cloud backup. Android's backup is switched off for the Game, so its data is not copied to Google Drive. For the same reason, progress and the Pigment balance cannot be recovered after the Game is uninstalled or on a new device. One-time purchases (No Ads Pack, Starter Pack) are restored from your Google account.
  • Usage statistics are kept in Firebase for 14 months at most and then deleted by Google.
  • Support emails are deleted six months after the request has been closed, unless we must keep a message longer to meet a legal obligation or to deal with a legal claim.
  • Order records are kept by Google in Google Play's developer console. Where we keep copies for accounting and tax purposes, we keep them for as long as the applicable law requires.
  • Information processed by Google AdMob is held by Google, not by us, for the periods set out in Google's own policies.

16. Security

  • Game data is kept encrypted in the Game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read on a standard Android device.
  • We do not operate servers or databases that hold information about users.
  • All connections made by the Game and its libraries are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Support emails are kept in a mailbox to which only we have access.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keeping the amount of information to a minimum is our main safeguard.

17. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over personal information about you.

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: you can ask for access to your personal data, for its correction or erasure, for the restriction of its processing and for data portability; you can object to processing that is based on legitimate interests; and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office).
  • Türkiye: your rights under Article 11 of KVKK and how to apply are described in the KVKK Notice. If we reject your request, if you find our answer insufficient or if we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).
  • United States: we do not sell your personal information. You can stop the use of the advertising ID for targeted advertising in your device's ad settings.
  • Other countries: we honour the rights that your local law gives you.

How to use your rights. Email or write to us using the details in section 20. We answer free of charge within 30 days. Because the Game has no accounts, usage data in Firebase is not linked to your name, and we usually cannot match a person to it. To stop the collection of usage data at once, switch off Settings → Usage statistics; if you also clear the Game's data, your device gets a new identifier and the old data is deleted at the end of the retention period. We can also act on emails you have sent us and, if you give us the order number, on order records. We may ask for the information we need to confirm that a request really comes from you. For information processed by Google, you can also contact Google as described in its Privacy Policy.
