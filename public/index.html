BerqWorks

BerqWorks is an independent studio that makes games and apps. Every step, from the first sketch to the store listing, happens at the same desk. Three Android games are on the way: a calm mahjong solitaire for grown-ups, a picture puzzle for children aged 3 to 8, and a sorting puzzle that brings faded scenes back to color.

None of them has a clock you can lose to. Every puzzle has a solution, and when you're stuck, the game tells you so.
